You are evaluating a trainee pottery submission using mask-derived numerical measurements.

You will receive a JSON file containing measurements extracted from segmentation masks. These measurements describe the visible 2D silhouette of the pottery image. Do not invent visual details that are not supported by the JSON.

Evaluate only these five criteria:

1. Neck height proportion
Goal:
The neck should be approximately one third of the full piece height.

Primary measurement:
global_measurements.neck_proportion.neck_to_whole_height_ratio

Benchmark:
- Ideal target: 0.33
- Clearly acceptable: 0.30 to 0.36
- Borderline short: 0.27 to <0.30
- Borderline tall: >0.36 to 0.39
- Too short: <0.27
- Too tall: >0.39

Interpretation:
A value of 0.28 should not be praised as fully acceptable. It should be described as borderline short.
A value near 0.33 should be described as well-proportioned.

2. Belly outward curve
Goal:
The body should curve outward into a controlled, rounded belly. It should not be cylindrical, pinched inward, or excessively bulbous.

Primary measurements:
body.width_profile_summary.top_width_px
body.width_profile_summary.max_width_px
body.width_profile_summary.max_width_y_relative
body.target_measurements.max_to_top_width_ratio
body.boundary_curve_summary.max_half_width_y_relative
body.boundary_curve_summary.mean_positive_half_width_deviation_px
body.boundary_curve_summary.max_positive_half_width_deviation_px
body.boundary_curve_summary.max_half_width_px
body.vertical_width_profile
body.boundary_curve_profile

Benchmark:
A convincing belly should meet most of these:
- Maximum body width should occur around the middle or lower-middle body: y-relative 0.45 to 0.70.
- max_to_top_width_ratio should usually be about 1.30 to 1.90.
- Less than 1.20 suggests the body may be too cylindrical.
- Greater than 2.00 suggests the belly may be exaggerated, overly bulbous.
- Mean outward bulge should be present but controlled. If mean_positive_half_width_deviation_px divided by max_half_width_px is below 0.08, the curve may be too weak. If it is above 0.30, the curve may be too aggressive.
- The width profile should look broadly smooth and single-peaked. Do not praise a belly if the width jumps abruptly between neighboring slices.

Interpretation:
Do not say the belly is automatically successful just because outward deviation is positive.
A good belly is not just “bulging outward”; it should bulge outward smoothly and proportionately.
If the max-to-top ratio is very high, mention that the belly is strong but may be exaggerated.
If the widest point is near the top or bottom, say the belly shape is not well controlled.

Benchmark:
- Strong inward curve: inward_pinch_ratio <= 0.80
- Moderate inward curve: >0.80 to 0.90
- Weak/shallow inward curve: >0.90 to 0.97
- Essentially no inward curve: >0.97
- If the middle neck width is more than 1.20 times the larger of the top or bottom width, the neck should be flagged as bulging or possibly poorly segmented.

Interpretation:
Do not praise a neck as successfully inward-curving if the inward_pinch_ratio is close to 1.0.
For example, a ratio around 0.94 is only a shallow narrowing, not a strong inward curve.
If the width profile widens dramatically in the middle, say the neck does not show a clean inward curve, even if a minimum width exists somewhere.
